How this version works

  • The number of joined rounds comes from the sphere’s pole-to-pole surface length and your round height.
  • Each round’s stitch count uses 2πr ÷ stitch width at that surface position.
  • The default conventional-chart model divides each completed round into equal angular sectors. It assumes the fabric redistributes shaping tension across the round rather than preserving rigid slanted stitch polygons.
  • Increase and decrease positions still determine the written crochet operations and the parent relationships, but they do not skew the geographic footprint of every later stitch in that row.
  • The optional centre-linked model places stitch centres from their parent attachments and constructs circular Voronoi-style footprints between neighbouring centre-lines.
  • When staggering is enabled, alternate increase rounds and alternate decrease rounds rotate their shaping points by half the spacing between them.
  • The comparison metrics show how sensitive the land coverage and final yarn decisions are to switching between this model and the inherited-edge model from v1.4. They do not constitute ground-truth accuracy.
  • The pole, roll, and seam controls rotate Earth relative to the crochet grid.

Practical note

The map is necessarily pixelated at crochet resolution. Check short one-stitch colour runs before committing to yarn changes.
